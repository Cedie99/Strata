Ask → Approve → Post

Your administrator decides which documents need a second pair of eyes and who looks at them, in which order. A document that needs approval is not posted when you save it: it waits as a request, and is posted only after the last approver says yes.

How it works

Saving a document checks the approval rules first. No rule matches: it is posted at once, as before.

  1. You save
    A PO, payment, void, journal…
    rules checked
  2. Request
    Numbered APR-…; nothing posted yet
    waiting
  3. Approvers
    Level 1, then level 2 …
    approvereject
  4. Posted
    After the last approval
    document numberstock / ledger
Nothing happens until it is approved.A waiting purchase order has no PO number, a waiting invoice does not take stock, a waiting cash out does not touch the cash book. A rejected or withdrawn request leaves no trace in the books, only in the approval history.

Each screen

Under the Approvals menu. The number on the menu is how many requests wait for you.

1

Waiting for my approval

Approvals → Waiting for my approval

Requests whose current step is yours: you hold the step's role, you did not ask for it, and you did not approve an earlier step. Open one to decide.

2

A request

Click a request number

What was submitted (the same figures and lines you would see on the document), why it needs approval, and each step with who decided, when and their note.

  • Approve (note optional). On the last step the button reads Approve and post: the document is posted at that moment, in the requester's name, and the request links to it.
  • Reject needs a reason, which the requester sees. Nothing is posted.
  • The requester can Withdraw request while it is still waiting.
  • If the document can no longer be posted when it is approved (for example, the stock is gone or the month is closed), the approval is refused with the reason and the request keeps waiting; reject it and submit a new one.
3

Requests

Approvals → Requests

Your requests, newest first, with their status (waiting, approved, rejected, withdrawn) and the posted document. With the permission "See every approval request", you see everyone's in your branches; filter by status.

4

Approval rules

Approvals → Approval rules (permission "Manage approval rules")

Name, When (one of the triggers above), the limit, and who approves: up to four roles, level 1 first. Untick Active to pause a rule.

  • A role marked ⚠ has no active user: requests would wait forever, so give the role to someone.
  • When several rules match one document, it needs all their approvers, each role once, in order.
  • Changing or deleting a rule affects new documents only; waiting requests keep the steps they were given.
5

On the document

Purchase orders, payments made, bills, invoices, journal entries

An Approvals box at the bottom shows the request that let it be posted (who approved, when) and any void or reversal still waiting.

Rules of the game

The controls auditors look for, enforced by the system.

RuleWhat it means
Maker-checkerThe person who asks can never approve their own request, even an administrator.
One person, one stepSomeone who approved level 1 cannot also approve level 2.
In orderLevel 2 sees nothing to do until level 1 has approved.
Permanent historyRequests and decisions are never deleted or edited (the database refuses), and every decision is in the audit log.
BranchesApprovers only see requests of branches they work in.
Still allowed?When it is finally posted, the requester must still have the permission to post that document.

How to

The common jobs, step by step.

Require approval for large POs

  1. Create the role (e.g. "Purchasing Manager") under Setup → Roles and give it to the right people.
  2. Approvals → Approval rules → New rule.
  3. When: Purchase order total; From amount: 50000; Level 1: Purchasing Manager.
  4. For a second signature above ₱500,000, add another rule with level 1 Purchasing Manager and level 2 Owner.

Approve or reject

  1. Click the number on the Approvals menu.
  2. Open the request and check the figures and lines.
  3. Approve, or reject with a reason.

Stop sales over a credit limit

  1. Set each customer's Credit limit on the customer page (0 = no limit).
  2. Add a rule: When "Customer over credit limit", level 1 Sales Manager.
  3. Invoices that would take a customer over their limit now wait.

My request is stuck

  1. Open it from Approvals → Requests to see which role it waits for.
  2. Ask someone with that role (not you) to look at it.
  3. If nobody has the role, ask the administrator; or withdraw it.
