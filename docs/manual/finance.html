Cash, books & reports

You rarely write accounting entries by hand in Strata. Sales, Purchasing and Inventory post them as they happen. Finance shows how the business is doing; Accounting keeps the general ledger, the statements and the month-end close.

Where the numbers come from

Documents post journal entries; the ledger adds them up; reports and the dashboard read the result.

  1. Documents
    Invoices, bills, payments, movements, cash book
    posted automatically
  2. Journal entries
    One balanced entry per document
    debits = credits
  3. General ledger
    Every account with a running balance
    per branch
  4. Statements
    Income statement, balance sheet, trial balance
    export to Excel

Finance

Under the Finance menu. For owners and managers.

1

Business dashboard

Finance → Business dashboard

The state of the business on one page, compared with the previous period. Pick the period: this month, last month, this quarter, this year or the last 12 months. It follows the branch picker.

  • Sales: revenue, gross profit and margin; top products and customers; sales by branch.
  • Receivables: what customers owe, by age, and the biggest debtors.
  • Cash: cash balance, money in and out, and 12 months of cash flow.
  • Inventory: stock value at cost and days of stock.
2

Cash book

Finance → Cash book

All money in and out of a branch. Customer payments and payments to suppliers appear here automatically; use New for everything else.

DirectionCategories you can enterAdded automatically
Cash inOpening balance, Owner capital, Loan received, Other incomeCustomer payments (from Sales)
Cash outPayroll, Rent & utilities, Taxes & fees, Loan repayment, Owner drawings, Other expense, Supplier paymentPayments to suppliers (from Purchasing), net of withholding
  • For suppliers with bills, pay through Purchasing → Payments made, not the "Supplier payment" category, so the bill is cleared and withholding is recorded.
  • Entries are never edited; correct a mistake with an opposite entry.

Accounting

Under the Accounting menu. For accountants and bookkeepers.

3

Reports

Accounting → Reports ► Income statement · Balance sheet · Trial balance · General ledger

Each report works per branch (title-bar picker) or for all your branches, and exports to Excel. In the general ledger every line links to the document that posted it, so you can trace any figure back to its invoice, bill or movement.

4

Journal entries

Accounting → Journal entries

Every entry, automatic and manual, numbered like JE-MNL-2026-000123. Use New for accruals, corrections and opening balances.

  • An entry must balance (debits = credits) and cannot be dated in a closed month.
  • Manual entries cannot touch receivables, inventory, payables or goods received not invoiced. Those balances come from their documents; change them through Sales, Inventory or Purchasing.
  • Posted entries are never edited. Open one and Reverse it, then post the correct entry.
5

6

Periods

Accounting → Periods

Close a month once it has ended and is checked. Months close in order. A closed month accepts no postings from any module. Reopening is possible for users with the permission and is recorded in the audit log.

What posts where

The entry each document writes. Dr = debit, Cr = credit.

Month-end, step by step

A simple routine that keeps the books ready for the BIR.

Before closing

  1. Make sure all invoices, bills, payments and cash entries for the month are posted.
  2. Check Purchasing → Receiving reports for goods "Not billed".
  3. Post accruals and corrections as manual journals.
  4. Run the trial balance; debits must equal credits.

Close and report

  1. Review the income statement and balance sheet per branch.
  2. Export them to Excel for your records.
  3. Accounting → Periods → close the month.
  4. Anything found later is corrected in the current open month.
