How the modules connect

Goods flow left to right: bought in Purchasing, held in Inventory, sold in Sales. Every step posts to the general ledger by itself. Finance reads the results; Setup decides who can do what.

The screen

The same layout on every page.

A

Title bar

Logo and system name (set by your administrator under Setup → Branding). On the right: the branch picker, your name (sign out here) and your roles.

  • The branch picker narrows every list, report and the dashboard to one branch. Choose "All branches" to see every branch you are assigned to.
  • New documents belong to the branch of the warehouse you choose on them.
B

Menu bar

One menu per module: Inventory, Sales, Purchasing, Finance, Accounting, Setup, plus any custom modules. You only see the menus and items your role allows. Some items sit in a ► submenu.

C

Home page

A shortcut to the business dashboard, a stock summary (active products, warehouses, products low on stock) and quick links to custom modules — again only what your role can open.

Lists and records

Every module uses the same list and record screens, so once you know one you know them all.

Find something in a list

  1. Type in the search box (numbers, names, references).
  2. Click a letter to jump to names starting with it.
  3. Open Filters for dates, status, branch and more.
  4. Use the funnel on a column heading for "contains", "greater than", date ranges.
  5. Click a row to open the record.

Export or import with Excel

  1. Export downloads exactly the rows you are seeing (search and filters applied).
  2. Import (products, warehouses, stock movements, custom records) offers a template to download.
  3. Fill in the template and upload it.
  4. An import is all or nothing: if one row is wrong, nothing is saved and every problem is listed.

Read a record

  1. List tab goes back to the list; General is the record itself.
  2. Attachments (products, warehouses, custom records) holds files up to 10 MB each.
  3. Document pages show "Accounting entries" at the bottom if you may see the ledger.

When a button is missing

  1. Your role does not include that permission.
  2. Or the document's state does not allow it (e.g. a paid invoice cannot be voided).
  3. Ask your administrator to adjust your role under Setup → Roles.

Printing and PDFs

Every document can be printed or saved as a PDF on your letterhead.

Print or save a document

  1. Open the document and click Print (or Print voucher). It opens in a new tab.
  2. Choose the paper: A4, Letter (short bond) or Legal (long bond). Strata remembers it on this computer.
  3. Click Print / Save as PDF. In the print window pick a printer, or "Save as PDF".

What is on every printout

  1. Your logo, company name, address, TIN, phone and email (Setup → Company settings and Branding).
  2. The document number, dates, party, lines and totals; amounts in words on money documents.
  3. Signature lines (prepared, checked, approved, received) and "printed by … on …" at the bottom.
  4. Page numbers when a document runs over one page.

Rules that apply everywhere

These keep the books trustworthy for auditors and the BIR. The database itself enforces them.

  1. 1
    Posted documents are never edited or deleted.

    Void an unpaid invoice or bill, reverse a journal, or post an adjustment. The original stays visible.

  2. 2
    Every change is recorded.

    The audit log keeps who did what and when, with the before and after. Its fingerprint chain shows if anyone tampers with it.

  3. 3
    Closed months stay closed.

    Once accounting closes a month, nothing can be dated in it. Post corrections in an open month.

  4. 4
    Numbers never skip.

    Invoice, bill and movement numbers are taken when you save, per branch and year, with no gaps.

  5. 5
    Stock never goes below zero.

    An issue, sale or transfer that would take more than is on hand is refused.

  6. 6
    Sign-ins last 7 days.

    After that you sign in again. Never share your password; every action is recorded under your name.
