Products, warehouses & stock

Inventory is the middle of Strata: Purchasing brings stock in, Sales takes it out, and every movement is valued in the books. Stock on hand is simply the sum of all movements.

How stock moves

Products and warehouses are the master data. Movements change the quantity. Stock on hand adds them up.

Each screen

Under the Inventory menu.

1

Products

Inventory → Products

SKU, name, unit (pc, L, kg, box…), cost, selling price, reorder level and description. Untick Active to retire a product; products are never deleted.

  • Cost is what one unit is worth in the books. Receiving on a purchase order updates it to the moving average automatically.
  • Changing the cost by hand revalues the stock on hand in the books (the difference goes to inventory adjustments).
  • Selling price is the default price on new sales invoices; it can be changed per invoice line.
  • Reorder level: at or below this quantity the product shows as "Low" on Stock on hand and on the home page.
  • Each product has an Attachments tab for specs, photos and certificates. Products can be imported from Excel.
2

Warehouses

Inventory → Warehouses

Code, name, address and the branch it belongs to. Users only see the warehouses of their own branches.

  • The warehouse decides the branch of every document that uses it: a sales invoice shipped from it, a PO delivered to it.
  • Untick Active to stop using a warehouse. Warehouses can be imported from Excel and have an Attachments tab.
3

Movements

Inventory → Movements

Every change in quantity, numbered like MOV-MNL-2026-000123 and dated the day it is posted. Four types:

TypeUse it forQuantityIn the books
ReceiptStock arriving without a purchase order; opening stock (reference OPENING)+Inventory against payables, or against opening equity for OPENING
IssueInternal use, samples, write-offs−Inventory adjustments
AdjustmentCount differences (type + or −)±Inventory adjustments
TransferMoving stock between warehouses− from, + to (one number)Nothing inside a branch; inter-branch clearing between branches
  • Movements made by sales invoices and receiving reports appear here too, with the document number as reference.
  • A movement is never edited. Fix a mistake with an opposite adjustment.
  • Click a row to open its printable slip (receipt, issue slip, adjustment or transfer slip) with signature lines.
  • Stock can never go below zero; an issue or transfer of more than is on hand is refused.
4

Stock on hand

Inventory → Stock on hand

Quantity per product and warehouse, with branch, unit and value at cost. Filter by branch, warehouse or stock level (low / above reorder level) and export to Excel.

Item types and item classesSetup → Inventory/WMS → Item Types and Item Classes hold your classification lists (e.g. Finished Goods › Chemicals › Degreaser). They are used by record types your administrator builds, such as an Item Master — see Custom modules.

How to

The common jobs, step by step.

Load opening stock

  1. Create the products and warehouses (or import them).
  2. Inventory → Movements → New, type Receipt.
  3. Enter the quantity and put OPENING as the reference.
  4. For many products, import a movements file instead.

Move stock to another warehouse

  1. Movements → New, type Transfer.
  2. Choose the product, the source warehouse and To warehouse.
  3. Enter the quantity and post. Both lines share one number.
  4. Click the movement in the list and print the transfer slip to go with the goods.

Record a physical count difference

  1. Compare the count with Stock on hand.
  2. Movements → New, type Adjustment.
  3. Enter the difference: positive if you found more, negative if less.
  4. Write the count sheet number in Reference.

Find what needs reordering

  1. Stock on hand → Filters → Stock level → Low.
  2. Export the list if you need to send it.
  3. Create purchase orders for those products (see Purchasing).

How Inventory connects

Inventory is fed and drawn down by other modules.

Other moduleWhat it does to Inventory
PurchasingReceiving a PO adds stock (a Receipt movement referenced by the receiving report) and updates each product's average cost.
SalesPosting an invoice issues the stock (one Issue per line, referenced by the invoice). Voiding it brings the stock back.
AccountingEvery movement and cost change posts a journal entry, so the Inventory account always equals quantity × cost.
FinanceThe dashboard shows inventory value at cost and days of stock.
SetupBranches decide who sees which warehouses; document numbering sets the movement number format.
