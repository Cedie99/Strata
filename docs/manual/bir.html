Where the figures come from

Four documents carry the tax. The BIR menu only reads them.

  1. Sales invoice
    VATable, zero-rated or exempt per line
    output VAT
  2. Payment received
    Did the customer withhold tax?
    CWT · 2307 in
  3. Supplier bill
    VAT type per line, goods or services
    input VAT
  4. Payment made
    Withholding deducted from the supplier
    EWT · 2307 out

VAT and withholding

Amounts on invoice and bill lines are always entered net of VAT; Strata adds the 12%.

TaxWhere you enter itAccountReported on

Input VATPurchase order and supplier bill lines (none from non-VAT suppliers)1300 Input VATPurchases book, SLP, 2550Q
Creditable withholding (CWT)Record payment: "Tax withheld (2307)" and ATC, suggested from the customer's rate1310 Creditable withholding taxCash receipts book, SAWT
Expanded withholding (EWT)Supplier bill: rate and ATC; deducted when you pay2200 Withholding taxes payableCash disbursements book, 1601-EQ, QAP, Form 2307
Example: an invoice of ₱1,000 net + ₱120 VAT = ₱1,120. The customer withholds 1% of the net (₱10) and pays ₱1,110. Record a payment of ₱1,120 applied with ₱10 tax withheld: the invoice is settled, cash goes up ₱1,110, and ₱10 waits as a tax credit until you claim it with their Form 2307.

Books of accounts

Under BIR ► Books of accounts. Pick a date range, then Export to Excel to print or bind.

1

General journal and general ledger

BIR → Books of accounts ► General journal · General ledger

The journal lists every entry line in date order with its source document. The ledger shows each account with its balance forward, every line in the period and a running balance (Dr / Cr).

2

Cash receipts and cash disbursements books

BIR → Books of accounts ► Cash receipts book · Cash disbursements book

Every entry that moves cash. Receipts show cash, creditable withholding and receivables in their own columns; disbursements show cash, withholding payable and payables. Anything else is listed under "Other accounts".

3

Sales book and purchases book

BIR → Books of accounts ► Sales book · Purchases book

Every invoice or supplier bill in the period with the customer's or supplier's TIN and address, split into exempt, zero-rated and VATable, with the VAT and total. Void documents stay in the list, marked VOID with no amounts, so the numbers have no gaps.

  • In the purchases book, VATable goods are billed receipts of stock; services are bill lines charged to an expense account.

How to

The quarterly routine.

Prepare the 2550Q

  1. Close the quarter's months after review (Accounting → Periods).
  2. BIR → 2550Q: check the ✓ lines, then copy the figures into the form.
  3. BIR → SLS and SLP: download the .DAT files and validate them.
  4. Attach the SAWT if customers withheld tax from you.

Withholding on suppliers

  1. Remit months 1 and 2 with 0619-E using the 1601-EQ worksheet's month columns.
  2. After the quarter, file 1601-EQ with the QAP from the same page.
  3. BIR → Form 2307: print one for each supplier and send it.

When a customer withholds tax

  1. Set their rate and ATC on the customer (e.g. 1%, WC158).
  2. Record the payment for the full amount applied; check the suggested tax withheld.
  3. Ask for their Form 2307 and file it; it backs the SAWT.

A figure does not match the ledger

  1. A ⚠ line means someone posted a manual journal to a VAT or withholding account.
  2. Find it in Accounting → General ledger for that account.
  3. Correct it with a reversal; tax accounts should only be moved by documents.
