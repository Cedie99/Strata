Sell → Collect

Sales turns stock into revenue and receivables, then into cash. One invoice issues the goods, records the sale and its VAT and fixes its gross margin; payments clear what the customer owes, including any tax they withhold.

Each screen

Under the Sales menu.

1

Customers

Sales → Customers

Code, name, email, phone, registered address, TIN and payment terms in days (0 = due on delivery). Untick Active to stop selling to a customer.

  • VAT on sales to them: VATable 12%, zero-rated (e.g. PEZA, export) or exempt. New invoice lines start with it.
  • They withhold (%) and Withholding ATC: if the customer deducts tax from payments and gives you Form 2307 (e.g. 1%, WC158), payments suggest that amount.
  • The TIN appears on printed invoices, the sales book, the SLS and the SAWT.
  • Credit limit: the most the customer may owe at once (0 = no limit). An approval rule can hold invoices that go over it.
  • The customer page shows what they owe, how much is overdue, and all their invoices with balances.
  • "New invoice" on the customer page starts an invoice with that customer filled in.
  • Statement of account on the customer page prints every open invoice with days overdue, an aging summary and the total due.
2

Invoices

Sales → Invoices → New

Choose the customer and the warehouse the goods ship from (it sets the invoice's branch), the date, an optional due date and reference (customer PO, DR number, official invoice number), then the lines: product, quantity, unit price and VAT.

  • Prices are net of VAT. Each line is VATable 12%, zero-rated or exempt; the form shows net, VAT 12% and total due.
  • The price starts at the product's selling price. The form shows the estimated gross profit (on the net amount) before you post.
  • Posting issues the stock and records each product's cost at that moment, so the margin on the invoice never changes later.
  • The due date defaults to the invoice date plus the customer's terms.
  • An invoice with no payments can be voided: the stock comes back and it stops counting as revenue. The number is kept.
  • Print gives an internal copy with your letterhead, the VAT breakdown (VATable, zero-rated, exempt sales, VAT), amount in words and a "received in good order" signature line.
Example: 5 drums at ₱150 net, VATable, cost ₱110 each → net ₱750, VAT ₱90, total due ₱840; cost of goods ₱550, gross profit ₱200 (26.7% of net).
3

Payments received

Open an invoice → Record payment · list under Sales → Payments received

Amount applied (what the invoice goes down by), date, tax withheld (2307) and its ATC, method (cash, check, bank transfer, card, other) and a reference such as the check number. Partial payments are fine; you can never record more than is still owed.

  • Tax withheld is suggested from the customer's rate on the net-of-VAT share of the amount; change it to what their Form 2307 says. The form shows the cash received = amount applied − tax withheld.
  • Tax withheld is not cash: it goes to Creditable withholding tax and appears on the SAWT. Keep the customer's Form 2307.
  • Each payment gets a number like PAY-MNL-2026-000001 and shows in the cash book as cash in (the cash part only).
  • In the invoice's Payments table, Print on a payment gives an acknowledgment receipt: amount applied, less tax withheld, cash received in words.
4

Receivables

Sales → Receivables

Every invoice with money still owed, oldest due date first, with days overdue. Filter by age (not due, 1–30, 31–60, 61–90, over 90 days) or branch. Open one to record a payment.

Official invoices.Strata's printed invoices are internal copies until it is registered with the BIR; official invoices still come from your registered system. Record its invoice number in Reference. Invoices posted before VAT on sales was added count as exempt sales with no VAT. See BIR for the sales book, SLS and SAWT.
May need approval.Sales invoices priced below cost, with a discount above the allowed percent, or that take a customer over their credit limit; and voiding an invoice. If your administrator has set an approval rule for it, saving sends it for approval instead of posting it: you land on the request, and it is posted only after the last approver says yes. See Approvals.

How to

The common jobs, step by step.

Invoice a customer

  1. Sales → Invoices → New (or "New invoice" on the customer).
  2. Pick the customer and the warehouse the goods leave from.
  3. Add lines; check the estimated gross profit.
  4. Post. The stock is issued and the invoice is numbered.

Record a payment

  1. Open the invoice (from Invoices or Receivables).
  2. In "Record payment", enter the amount applied, date and method; check the tax withheld.
  3. Add the check or transfer number in Reference.
  4. Record it. The balance and status update at once.

Cancel a wrong invoice

  1. Open the invoice. It must have no payments.
  2. Click Void invoice and confirm. If voids need approval, it waits until approved.
  3. The stock is received back; post a corrected invoice.
  4. If its month is closed, ask accounting: the correction belongs in an open month.

Chase overdue accounts

  1. Sales → Receivables → Filters → Age.
  2. Export the list for collection calls.
  3. Open a customer and click Statement of account; print it or save it as a PDF to send.

How Sales connects

What a sale changes elsewhere.

Other moduleEffect
InventoryOne Issue movement per invoice line, referenced by the invoice number; a void receives it back.
AccountingInvoice: receivable / sales + output VAT, and cost of goods sold / inventory. Payment: cash + creditable withholding / receivable. A void reverses the invoice's entry.
FinanceRevenue (net of VAT), gross profit and margin, receivables aging, top customers and products, sales by branch; the cash part of payments counts as cash in.
BIRSales book, cash receipts book, SLS, 2550Q (output VAT) and SAWT (tax withheld by customers).
PurchasingThe cost used for gross margin is the average cost built up by receiving purchase orders.
