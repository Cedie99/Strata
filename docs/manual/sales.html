Sell → Collect

Sales turns stock into revenue and receivables, then into cash. One invoice issues the goods, records the sale and fixes its gross margin; payments clear what the customer owes.

Each screen

Under the Sales menu.

1

Customers

Sales → Customers

Code, name, email, phone, address and payment terms in days (0 = due on delivery). Untick Active to stop selling to a customer.

  • The customer page shows what they owe, how much is overdue, and all their invoices with balances.
  • "New invoice" on the customer page starts an invoice with that customer filled in.
2

Invoices

Sales → Invoices → New

Choose the customer and the warehouse the goods ship from (it sets the invoice's branch), the date, an optional due date and reference (customer PO, DR number), then the lines: product, quantity and unit price.

  • The price starts at the product's selling price. The form shows the estimated gross profit before you post.
  • Posting issues the stock and records each product's cost at that moment, so the margin on the invoice never changes later.
  • The due date defaults to the invoice date plus the customer's terms.
  • An invoice with no payments can be voided: the stock comes back and it stops counting as revenue. The number is kept.
Example: 5 drums at ₱150, cost ₱110 each → total ₱750, cost of goods ₱550, gross profit ₱200 (26.7%).
3

Payments received

Open an invoice → Record payment · list under Sales → Payments received

Amount, date, method (cash, check, bank transfer, card, other) and a reference such as the check number. Partial payments are fine; you can never record more than is still owed.

  • Each payment gets a number like PAY-MNL-2026-000001 and shows in the cash book as cash in.
4

Receivables

Sales → Receivables

Every invoice with money still owed, oldest due date first, with days overdue. Filter by age (not due, 1–30, 31–60, 61–90, over 90 days) or branch. Open one to record a payment.

VAT on sales is not in Strata yet.Invoice totals are recorded as entered. Official BIR invoices still come from your registered system; record its invoice number in Reference. Sales VAT and customer withholding (Form 2307) are on the roadmap.

How to

The common jobs, step by step.

Invoice a customer

  1. Sales → Invoices → New (or "New invoice" on the customer).
  2. Pick the customer and the warehouse the goods leave from.
  3. Add lines; check the estimated gross profit.
  4. Post. The stock is issued and the invoice is numbered.

Record a payment

  1. Open the invoice (from Invoices or Receivables).
  2. In "Record payment", enter the amount, date and method.
  3. Add the check or transfer number in Reference.
  4. Record it. The balance and status update at once.

Cancel a wrong invoice

  1. Open the invoice. It must have no payments.
  2. Click Void invoice and confirm.
  3. The stock is received back; post a corrected invoice.
  4. If its month is closed, ask accounting: the correction belongs in an open month.

Chase overdue accounts

  1. Sales → Receivables → Filters → Age.
  2. Export the list for collection calls.
  3. Open a customer for their full balance.

How Sales connects

What a sale changes elsewhere.

Other moduleEffect
InventoryOne Issue movement per invoice line, referenced by the invoice number; a void receives it back.
AccountingInvoice: receivable / sales, and cost of goods sold / inventory. Payment: cash / receivable. A void reverses the invoice's entry.
FinanceRevenue, gross profit and margin, receivables aging, top customers and products, sales by branch; payments count as cash in.
PurchasingThe cost used for gross margin is the average cost built up by receiving purchase orders.
